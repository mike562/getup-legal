Getup · Halloween Edition

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Getup ("the app") is a costume try-on app. This policy explains what the app does with your information, in plain language. The short version: your photos are processed to make your costume image and are never stored on our servers. Your looks live on your phone.

Photos

  • When you generate a costume look, the photo you choose (and, if you use them, a costume photo or a snapshot of a product tag) is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to Google's Gemini API to create your costume image.
  • Right after each look is made, your photo and the generated look are sent the same way to build the look's shopping list, so it covers only what the costume adds, not clothes you are already wearing.
  • Our servers never store your photos or your generated images. They are processed in memory and discarded when the request finishes, except where the law requires us to preserve and report content (for example, suspected child sexual abuse material).
  • Google's handling: Google processes these images as our service provider to return the result. Under the paid Gemini API terms we use, Google does not use them to train its models. Google may keep request data (including the photo) for up to 55 days solely to detect abuse and policy violations, and its staff may review requests flagged for possible abuse.
  • No biometric identifiers: we do not create, collect, store, or retain faceprints, face geometry, or any biometric templates. Photos are used only as pixels for the request you made.
  • Only upload photos of people who are okay with it. For children's photos, that decision belongs to their parent or guardian.

On your phone

  • Each look you make is saved in the app's own storage on your phone, together with the photo you used, so you can reopen, reshop and remix it. These files are encrypted by iOS while your phone is locked, and they are included in your phone's own backups (iCloud or computer).
  • You can delete a look, or all looks at once, from My Looks. About → Delete my data deletes all of them along with your data on our server.
  • Photos are resized and re-encoded before upload, which removes location and other metadata from the file.

What we store on our servers

  • A random device identifier created on first launch, used to track your credit balance and purchases. It is not your name, email, phone number, or advertising ID.
  • A credit ledger: purchases, free credits, share bonuses, and promo-code redemptions tied to that device identifier.
  • Purchase verification data from Apple when you buy credits (the signed transaction record; payment itself is handled entirely by Apple, and we never see your payment details).
  • Content reports: if you report a look, we keep the reason you chose (for example "inappropriate") with your device identifier for up to 90 days, for safety review. When you report a look as inappropriate you can choose to send the image with the report. If the look includes a child, we keep that image encrypted for a child-safety review (below); otherwise we don't keep it.
  • Child-safety reviews: if our safety systems flag a request that involves a child as possible sexual exploitation, we keep an encrypted copy of that request (the photos and text) so a person can review it; depending on the signal, that device may be paused from making new looks until the review is done. Lower-risk signals keep only the costume text and a note of what was flagged, not photos. If the review finds apparent child sexual abuse material, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as US law requires and keep it for one year; otherwise we delete it.
  • Rate-limit counters: short-lived counters keyed by a one-way, secret-keyed hash of your device identifier or network address. Raw addresses are never stored, and counters expire within a day or two.
  • Server logs: short-lived operational logs (kept by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, for a few days) may include your random device identifier and events such as "look generated". They never include photos, generated images, or the text you type.

Costume text, ideas and scans

  • Text you type (costume descriptions, quiz answers, interests) is sent to our server and to Google's Gemini API to generate ideas, shopping suggestions, or your costume image. Don't include personal information in these fields.
  • When you scan a costume's barcode, the barcode number is looked up in product databases (such as UPCitemdb) and with Google Search to identify the product. Barcodes identify products, not people. We keep daily counts of lookups, and the barcode and product found appear in our short-lived server logs, to improve the scanner.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no email collection, no contact upload.
  • No advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no trackers, no advertising ID.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No server-side storage of photos or generated images, except the encrypted child-safety copies described above.

Shopping and paid links

"Shop this look" opens retailer searches (such as HalloweenCostumes.com, Spirit Halloween, Amazon and Walmart) in the retailer's app or your browser, outside Getup. Some are affiliate (paid) links: if you buy through them, the retailer pays Getup a commission at no extra cost to you. The app labels them where they appear. Getup counts how many times each retailer's button is tapped per day, as a total only; nothing about who tapped is recorded. Retailers and their affiliate networks may use cookies on their own sites under their own privacy policies.

Children

Getup is for adults 18 and older; the app asks you to confirm your age the first time you open it. Where the law requires it, the app asks Apple for your age range instead (Apple's Declared Age Range): the app only learns whether you're 18 or older, and keeps nothing but that yes on your phone. It is not directed at children. The app has no accounts and collects no personal information; photos of children may only be uploaded by a parent or guardian who consents to the processing described above.

Data retention and deletion

  • Delete my data (in the app under About) deletes your saved looks on this phone and, on our server, your credit history, reports and balance. Any remaining credits are lost. We keep only the bare device identifier with a note that its free looks were already granted and which promo codes it used (so they can't be claimed twice), and the anonymous Apple transaction numbers of past purchases (so a purchase can't be redeemed twice).
  • Deleting the app removes your saved looks from this phone. The random device identifier stays in your phone's keychain so your credits are still there if you reinstall; use Delete my data to remove your server data.
  • Content reports are deleted after 90 days; rate-limit counters within a day or two.

Do Not Track

The app doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, so browser "Do Not Track" signals don't change anything it does.
