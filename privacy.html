Getup · Halloween Edition

Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Getup ("the app") is a costume try-on app. This policy explains what the app does with your information, in plain language. The short version: your photos are processed to make your costume image and are never stored on our servers.

Photos

  • When you generate a costume look, the photo you choose (and, if you use them, a costume photo or a snapshot of a product tag) is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to **Google's Gemini API** to create your costume image.
  • Photos are processed in memory only. **We do not store your photos or your generated images on our servers.** Your generated looks are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage, and you can delete them at any time from My Looks.
  • No biometric identifiers: we do not create, collect, store, or retain faceprints, face geometry, or any biometric templates. Photos are used only as pixels for a one-time image generation.
  • Google processes the image data as our service provider to generate the result. We do not permit our providers to use your photos to train their models where that control is offered.
  • Only upload photos of people who are okay with it. For children's photos, that decision belongs to their parent or guardian.

What we store

  • A random device identifier created on first launch, used to track your credit balance and purchases. It is not your name, email, phone number, or advertising ID, and we cannot link it to you.
  • A credit ledger: purchases, free credits, share bonuses, and promo-code redemptions tied to that device identifier.
  • Purchase verification data from Apple when you buy credits (we verify the signed transaction; payment itself is handled entirely by Apple — we never see your payment details).

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no email collection, no contact upload.
  • No advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No server-side storage of photos or generated images.

Rate limiting

To keep the service healthy, our servers keep short-lived, hashed request counters (for example, a hashed form of a network address). Raw addresses are not stored.

Costume text and ideas

Text you type (costume descriptions, quiz answers, interests) is sent to our server and to Google's Gemini API to generate ideas, shopping suggestions, or your costume image. Don't include personal information in these fields.

Shopping links

"Shop this look" opens retailer search pages (Amazon, Etsy, Google Shopping) inside the app. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Links may include an affiliate tag that identifies the app, not you.

Children

Getup is not directed at children under 13. The app has no accounts and collects no personal information; photos of children may only be uploaded by a parent or guardian who consents to the processing described above.

Data retention and deletion

There is nothing personal to delete on our side: photos are never stored, and the device identifier is random. Deleting the app removes your saved looks and your device's local data. If you want your device's credit ledger row removed from our database, contact us with the request from within the app.

Changes

We'll update this policy as the app evolves and change the effective date above. Material changes will be noted in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: use About → Send feedback in the app, or email the address listed there.
