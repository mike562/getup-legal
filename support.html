Support

Help with the Getup app for iPhone

Contact us

The fastest way to reach us is inside the app: About → Send feedback (the info icon on the home screen). It pre-fills your app version so we can help quickly. We read everything.

Common questions

How do credits work?

1 credit = 1 generated costume image, in every mode. Your first 3 looks are free, you can earn bonus credits by sharing, and credit packs are available in the app. Credits never expire.

A generation failed — was I charged?

No. If a generation fails, the credit is automatically refunded to your balance. If your balance ever looks wrong, contact us via Send feedback.

How do refunds work?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your device. Photos are processed in memory to generate your image and are never stored on our servers — see the Privacy Policy. Deleting the app deletes your saved looks.

How do I report a generated image?

Every result screen has a flag icon — one tap sends us a report. For rights concerns, use Send feedback.
